AlphaFolio

AlphaFolio is a personal portfolio tracker for families. It brings your stock accounts and bond holdings into one place, so you can see net worth, profit and loss, bond payouts and allocation for each family member or for everyone together.

What it does

  • Track stock accounts and bonds for each family member.
  • See bond payout schedules, maturities and category breakdowns.
  • Back up your data, encrypted, to your own Google Drive.

Your data stays yours

Your portfolio data is stored on your device. Backups go only to a private app folder in your own Google Drive account. See the Privacy Policy.
