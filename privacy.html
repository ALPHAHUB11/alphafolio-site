AlphaFolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

AlphaFolio is a personal portfolio tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data the app stores

The app stores the portfolio information you enter or connect: family member names, stock account details, holdings, transactions, bond records and app settings. This data is stored on your device.

Broker credentials

If you connect a broker, your API keys, PINs and TOTP secrets are kept only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). They are never included in backups and are never sent to the developer.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to back up, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests access to the Google Drive App Data scope (drive.appdata). This gives the app access only to its own hidden folder in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

Backup files are created on your device and stored in that folder in your own Google account. Backups are encrypted on your device with a passphrase that only you know, before they are uploaded.

What we collect and share

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. The app has no analytics or advertising. Your data goes only between your device and your own Google Drive.

Google API Services

AlphaFolio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete the app to remove its data from your device. To remove backups, delete the app's data from your Google Account settings under "Third-party apps & services", or revoke AlphaFolio's access there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
